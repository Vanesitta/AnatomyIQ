Learning human anatomy is exceptionally complex, often overwhelming students with a massive barrage of clinical jargon, intricate cellular diagrams, and dense medical textbook information that hinders their initial conceptual understanding. To address this educational challenge, AnatomyIQ leverages the Computational Thinking pillar of Abstraction, strategically filtering out excessive clinical noise to focus strictly on pedagogical essentials that promote long-term retention. By applying this abstraction, the platform models the intricate, multi-layered realities of the human body as a simplified and highly structured hierarchy, organizing vast anatomical systems down into individual organs, key biological facts, and targeted review quizzes. This systematic reduction of clinical detail effectively prevents cognitive overload, allowing learners to efficiently build a solid foundational understanding of major bodily systems without getting bogged down in diagnostic complexities. Ultimately, AnatomyIQ delivers an interactive, full-stack anatomy education web application that integrates system-by-system browsing, organ-specific details, gamified quizzes, and personal health tracking to provide a highly structured and manageable learning pathway for students.
